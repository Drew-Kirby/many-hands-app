Many Hands

A volunteer community app: post issues, break them into tasks, and see who's on what.
Flutter mobile app by Drew Kirby, CS 403 Mobile App Development, SVSU.

Android

Download the Android app (APK)
  1. Tap the button above on your Android phone.
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installing apps from your browser ("Install unknown apps").
  3. Tap Install, then open Many Hands.

iPhone

Open the iPhone version
  1. Open this page in Safari and tap the button above.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Many Hands from your home screen. It runs full screen like an app.

The same link works in any desktop browser too.

What's inside

  • Ask AI: search issues, tasks and members in plain English ("unclaimed tasks due this week"), powered by Gemini.
  • Voice-to-text: tap the mic on any search or text box and speak instead of typing.
  • Home with your tasks, unclaimed tasks and issues that need people.
  • Issues with search, Active / Resolved / All filters and sorting; tasks you can take, update, give back and comment on.
  • Members with search and each person's workload.
  • Stress tested with 21 members, 60 issues and 255 tasks.
